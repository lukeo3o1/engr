Planning metadata: what is grouped together. Nothing here is confirmed

Usage: engr-current collection [OPTIONS] <COMMAND>

Commands:
  new       Start a plan under an id you choose
  ls        List plans
  show      Show one plan, its schedule and its members
  rename    Replace the title
  describe  Replace the description. Omit --text to clear it
  state     Declare where the plan stands
  schedule  Replace the schedule. Give none of the dates to clear it
  add       Put something in the plan
  rm        Take something out of the plan
  order     Rank a member, or omit --order to unrank it
  priority  Set a member's priority, or omit --priority to clear it
  delete    Delete the whole plan. Only on explicit human direction
  help      Print this message or the help of the given subcommand(s)

Options:
      --attempt <N>  Which attempt of your own review this is, counted from 1. Needed only where a project rule governs collection [default: 1]
      --root <ROOT>  Workspace root. Defaults to the nearest ancestor containing .engr
